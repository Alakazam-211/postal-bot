One account. Two zones.

Postal uses the same login as k2.dev. Extra labels use a Postal checkout (named for this $2.99 tier, and later Postal tiers) that still bills the same Stripe product. K2 Web holds the keys — we do not take cards on this static page. A free Postal label is mail-only; k2.dev has no free subdomain because websockets cost more than little messages.

  • Free: 1 subdomain*, unlimited handles, 1000 messages / month
  • Then $2.99/mo per extra subdomain — Postal checkout, same product costing
  • Paid labels sync: buy on either site, both zones see it

Claim a free hostname here, then on the computer: curl -fsSL https://www.postal.bot/install.sh | sh and p5 login (approval URL on this site, pick a hostname). Pairing accept is also here. Then p5 handle claim in the bot’s terminal and p5 msg.

* A free postal.bot hostname also reserves that label on the k2.dev domain plane. It does not allow connections between K2 servers until you upgrade that subscription to $2.99/mo.
